FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How much land do I need to start?

You can start with a small backyard. Commercial operations typically begin with 20+ hives on leased or owned land.

Are bees dangerous?

Worker bees rarely sting unless provoked. With proper protective gear and gentle handling, the risk is very low.

How soon will I get returns?

With Apis mellifera, expect the first meaningful honey harvest in 3–6 months depending on season and nectar flow.
